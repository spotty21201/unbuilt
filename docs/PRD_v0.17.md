# Unbuilt — Feature 12: Terrain, drainage and phasing

Authorized 30 October 2026. The owner's Feature 12 numbering supersedes historical roadmap numbering. Preserve earlier PRDs, all uncommitted work and the deferred Feature 11 tester prompt. This is an initial planning calculation tool with entered supporting data; it does not establish civil-engineering or financial suitability.

Open Terrain, drainage and phasing beside Feasibility experiments, including on a boundary-only/blank workspace. The native review dialog holds accepted geometry and base cost assumptions. Edit the three independent study groups, inspect maps and calculations, compare accepted versus draft results, then Apply development inputs or Cancel. One accepted input change is one plan Undo/Redo step. Unapplied inputs are session-only. Opening a review supplies no project data or numerical result automatically. Fictional examples require explicit selection and stay in the draft until Apply.

## Terrain

- A complete rectangular grid of x,y,z points, already in local Cartesian metres and the plan's origin/datum: 2–20 distinct x and y coordinates, 4–400 points, no missing/duplicate cells. Spacing is at least 0.01 m. x/y use the supported 0–500 m workspace, z is bounded to −500–9,000 m. Equal grid spacing is not required. A declared source, vertical datum, real calendar observation date and assumed/user-declared-survey basis are required. Survey declarations are not independently verified.
- Import or paste a bounded UTF-8 CSV with exact `x,y,z` header and numeric rows. No inference of latitude/longitude, coordinate conversion, automatic alignment, GeoTIFF/lidar or irregular point-cloud import. Source data must be prepared for this frame first.
- Each cell splits SW–NE into two planar triangles. The actual simple site polygon is triangulated and clips those facets; concave remainders are excluded. No extrapolation is used. Insufficient coverage withholds terrain results, including after a boundary resize/change; original source levels are retained.
- Elevation extrema, area-weighted and maximum slope, and area above an explicitly entered threshold are measured on the clipped surface. Thresholds are screening assumptions, not buildability standards. A map shows elevation shading, threshold edges and sampled local downhill gradients; these are not drainage routes.
- An optional whole-site platform elevation yields separate gross geometric cut and fill. Integration splits facets at the zero-height contour before summing each side. This is a deliberately simple platform sensitivity, not a grading proposal. It excludes breaklines, local pads, retaining structures, topsoil, bulking, compaction, transport and earthworks costs. No lots or streets are automatically moved/excluded.

## Drainage

- Enter sources for storm/runoff/concentration assumptions, return-period metadata, rainfall intensity (mm/h), intensity duration (minutes), time of concentration (minutes), and four runoff coefficients C (0–1). No local rainfall or soil properties are inferred.
- Explicitly declare that the site boundary is the whole contributing catchment, with no off-site inflow, and that no significant storage affects its peak response. Otherwise the Rational screening result is unavailable. Duration must cover the entered concentration time. The method is bounded to 80 ha; current supported sites are smaller.
- Disjoint measured surface areas are home footprints, street surface including the access apron, true designed green, and all remaining site land. Lot area is not treated as impervious roof area. C is area weighted and the SI calculation is `Q = Σ(C × A_m²) × I_mm/h / 3,600,000` m³/s.
- An optional mapped outfall reference has entered position, capacity (m³/s) and source. Compare the calculated peak with that declared capacity; this does not prove hydraulic connection, permission, downstream performance or compliance.
- Gross rainfall volume is separately `A × I × duration / 60 / 1,000` m³ before losses. It is never labeled runoff volume or detention storage. No network routing, pipe sizing, infiltration, sink/flood analysis, hydrograph, detention design or statutory compliance is established. The result describes the entered uniform storm, not an optimized critical-duration design storm.

## Phasing

- Enter a schedule source, start month, owner-benchmark payment month and 1–8 named phases. Each has build start/duration, a sale month at/after completion, and a shared non-land cost percentage. Horizon is bounded to 240 months. Infrastructure-only phases can have no homes.
- Assign every occupied lot exactly once through the map or accessible lot table. Assignments use stable lot rectangles. Vacancies are not homes. Missing, removed, reshaped/replaced or newly generated references leave cash unavailable until explicit remapping; nothing is silently assigned or deleted. A draft can clear obsolete references and bulk-assign unassigned current homes to a selected phase.
- Each phase receives its assigned homes' true template construction cost plus that construction's contingency. The rest of accepted non-land costs follows explicit shares totaling 100%. This schedules the existing budget: it does not add or double-count terrain/drainage allowances. Approved additional works must be entered separately through existing cost review.
- Costs spread evenly across each build period. All receipts and selling expenses fall in its declared sale month. The owner benchmark is paid once at the selected month. Monthly receipts, non-land spending, selling spending, land payment, net and cumulative cash are inspectable. Closing cash must reconcile to accepted modeled surplus. Target surplus is a planning benchmark, not a cash expense.
- Report maximum **month-end deficit**, not an actual financing requirement. No intramonth funding, absorption, escalation, tax, interest/debt, discounted return, phased access/utility/drainage dependencies or approvals are verified. Missing accepted budgets remain missing.

## Persistence and integration

`SiteFrame.development` carries optional, independent terrain/drainage/phasing inputs. Strict shape/unit/number/reference validation runs at imports and calculation boundaries. Structurally valid observations can be retained while coverage, applicability or lot assignments need review; affected calculations remain unavailable. Malformed/empty numeric fields do not become zero and cannot be applied. Removal of a study is explicit and undoable.

Project v13, decision record v14 and snapshot v11 plus `terrain-drainage-phasing` carry the new inputs, including studies existing only in saved alternatives. Older disguised envelopes reject. All legacy project formats remain readable. Full input precision participates in scenario hashes. Projects, portable records, named schemes and Undo/Redo retain sources and observations; these are data, not original survey media.

Boundary replacement, resizing, starter regeneration and clear/delete retain the inputs and recheck their relevance; they never relocate a survey or automatically reassign lots. Saved alternatives compare independent study results and expose each scheme's own sources/assumptions. Feasibility trial review reports study consequences and stale phase references before geometry Apply. Deterministic server explanations carry the same findings. AI receives measured screening statements and applicability limits only, excluding raw grids and source text; its tools cannot alter these studies.

Validation uses analytic surfaces, disjoint-area/flow arithmetic, exact construction and monthly cash reconciliation, malformed imports and lifecycle/real-browser checks. Fictional tests are not real survey, hydrology, financial or independent planner acceptance. No project elevation, storm or phase data has been supplied by the owner at implementation time. Feature 11 real-model access also remains deferred until routing and secrets arrive.
